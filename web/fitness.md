## gym exercises for back and thigh for fast strength and metabolism
Here are the best gym exercises for targeting the back and thighs: 

1\. The Deadlift (Barbell or Trap Bar)

-   **Muscles targeted:** Entire back, glutes, and hamstrings (back of the thighs).

-   **Why it works:** Deadlifts engage almost every muscle in the body, forcing maximum force production and acting as a metabolic powerhouse.

-   **How to do it:** Position your midfoot under the bar with your feet hip-width apart. Hinge at the hips and bend your knees to grip the bar. Drive through your heels, extend your hips, and stand tall while keeping your spine neutral. [](https://www.goldsgym.com/blog/10-best-back-exercises-for-building-muscle/)
    
2\. Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs)

-   **Muscles targeted:** Lower back, glutes, and hamstrings. 
-   **Why it works:** RDLs are fantastic for building serious strength in the posterior chain and are heavily associated with elevating the metabolic rate. 

-   **How to do it:** Stand holding dumbbells or a barbell. Keeping a slight bend in your knees, push your glutes back while keeping your back perfectly straight and chest up. Lower the weights until you feel a deep stretch in your hamstrings, then drive your hips forward to return to standing. [](https://www.anytimefitness.com/blog/learn-how-to-strength-train-your-back-and-legs-at-the-same-time)

3\. Goblet Squats

-   **Muscles targeted:** Quadriceps (front of thighs), hamstrings, glutes, and upper/lower back. [](https://www.health.harvard.edu/exercise-and-fitness/effective-exercises-for-building-a-strong-back)

-   **Why it works:** It is an excellent full-body metabolic move that is easy to load safely. Holding the weight in front of your chest forces your back muscles to work to keep your torso upright. [](https://www.hevyapp.com/compound-isolation-leg-exercises/)

-   **How to do it:** Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell vertically against your chest. Squat down by pushing your hips back and bending your knees, making sure to keep your chest lifted. Push through your heels to return to the starting position. [](https://www.health.harvard.edu/exercise-and-fitness/effective-exercises-for-building-a-strong-back)
 
4\. Kettlebell Swings

-   **Muscles targeted:** Glutes, hamstrings, and dynamically activates all back muscles. [](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gld5_qx3F4E&t=663)
    

-   **Why it works:** This is a dynamic, explosive plyometric movement that demands Rate of Force Production, making it incredible for metabolic conditioning. [](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gld5_qx3F4E&t=663)

-   **How to do it:** Stand with feet wider than shoulder-width apart. Keeping a soft bend in your knees, hinge your hips back and swing the kettlebell between your legs. Forcefully thrust your hips forward to swing the weight up to chest height.
